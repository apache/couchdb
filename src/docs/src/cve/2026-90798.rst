===========================================================================
CVE-2026-90798: Replicator follows redirects indefinitely
===========================================================================

:Date: 12.10.2026

:Affected: 3.5.2 and below

:Severity: Medium

:Vendor: The Apache Software Foundation

Description
===========

The CouchDB replicator follows HTTP redirects indefinitely. This allowed a
malicious replication endpoint to cause a denial of service for individual
replication jobs.

Mitigation
==========

Existing retry limits are now applied for all replicator requests. After 5
retries the replication job fails with a ``too_many_redirects error``.

Workarounds
===========

N/A

Credit
======

The CouchDB team discovered this issue while reviewing independent reports from
Piotr Karwasz, Sec-Rex & ZeroVuln Labs.
